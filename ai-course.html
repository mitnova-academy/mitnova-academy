Course curriculum

Six modules that take you from the basics to your first practical AI project.

  1. Module 1

    Understanding Artificial Intelligence

    What AI is, where it came from, and the everyday examples you already use.

  2. Module 2

    How AI Works

    Data, patterns and learning explained in simple words, with no maths needed.

  3. Module 3

    Understanding AI Tools

    Explore popular AI assistants and tools, and learn how to choose the right one.

  4. Module 4

    Prompt Engineering

    Write clear instructions that get useful, accurate answers from AI.

  5. Module 5

    AI for Business and Work

    Use AI to save time on writing, research, planning and customer service.

  6. Module 6

    Your First Practical AI Project

    Bring everything together by completing a small project of your own.

What You Will Learn

By the end of this course you will be able to:

  • Explain what AI is and how it works in simple terms
  • Spot AI in everyday life, work and business
  • Use popular AI tools with confidence
  • Write clear prompts that give better results
  • Use AI responsibly: check facts, protect privacy and avoid misuse
  • Complete a practical project that shows what you can do
